PEACHES ensemble-size and ChEES covariance audit

Gaussian protocol: D=32/64, seeds 0/1, N=2D/2D+2/2D+4/4D,200 warmup/500 production,
float32, public defaults (target_accept=.651, max_L=20). Starts are nested prefixes
of normal(key(42+seed),(4D,D)). Results include source/init hashes, actual logical
production gradient costs, moments, observable ESS, ranks and wall time.

All 16 current-complement cells were finite and accurate: variance RMS error
.0055-.0114, minimum observable ESS 111-182 of 500 ensemble averages. Thus N=2D
worked in this Gaussian protocol despite a singular complement warmup metric.
Extra 2/4 walkers did not uniformly help. N=2D and N=4D gradient budgets were similar
because learned trajectories were roughly twice as long at N=2D.

Four full-current-covariance N=2D ablations improved estimated minimum equivalent
draws per million production gradients:
                   complement    full
  D=32 seed=0           26626      29515
  D=32 seed=1           28504      30777
  D=64 seed=0           21826      26257
  D=64 seed=1           16943      25346
Variance RMS stayed .006-.012. These four gains are empirical, not a universal
promise of better efficiency or robustness.

The narrow proposal uses cov(concat(current_group,complement)) only when W<=D
and total N>D. A centered half covariance has rank at most W-1, so its inverse is
undefined at N=2D. Otherwise the existing complement metric is retained. Full
covariance and its scalar score transform correctly under invertible affine
maps. Collinear/global-rank-deficient walkers gain no artificial rank or ridge.
The production kernels stay unchanged. All 3 helpers preserve W>D bitwise helper
and whole-sampler behavior; W=D affine invariance and rank checks pass.

Full-always covariance is a separate experiment. Eight matched Rosenbrock
N=64, D=2/8,seeds 0/1 cells (shared stretch 100+100 preparation,750/3000, float32,
max_L=20) have mixed accuracy/efficiency results. The narrow fallback leaves
these W=32>D cases unchanged. Staged/rate policies remain separate proposals.

ESS comes from ensemble-mean coordinate/square traces with Sokal windows and
IAT clamped at 1. Equivalent draws multiply by N; they do not certify independent
walkers or convergence. Wall time includes compilation/warmup. Always inspect
moment errors and observable stability alongside cost.

The conditional fallback and its public regression tests have been applied to
the maintained main and astro branches. reference/ keeps immutable pre-fallback
sources; proposal/ keeps the exact fallback sources with the existing API. The
source/test patches preserve the reviewed change for audit. Full-always metric,
staged warmup and cost-rate policies remain isolated experiments.
verify_proposal.py checks identity/affine/rank properties; the public test patch
also compares the gradient with an independently differentiated flow criterion.
results.json,rosen_results.json and run.json hold measurements/provenance.
benchmark_gaussian.py and run_gaussian_grid.py replay the exact Gaussian protocol.
